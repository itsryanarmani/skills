# Patterns

Counts are pages out of 54, from `tools/aggregate_teardowns.py`. Frequency is not effectiveness: a pattern can be common and still not help. The "why" column is a mechanism to test, not a finding. Counts marked (h) use a keyword heuristic in the script.

## Length and price

Pages that show a paid price have a median of 4.8 screens (n=15). Pages that show none have 3.3 (n=39). The link is weak. A $1,297 page (`think-media-youtube-cohort`) is 4.3 screens. Pages with no visible price run from 0.5 screens (`siddharth-rajsekar-ai-coaching-workshop`) to 11.9 (`persuasion-experience-performance-agency`).

## Read this before you copy

- Pages here are shown by the gallery, not sampled. The set favours well-known creators.
- Common does not mean it converts. No page has a conversion number.
- Patterns 7, 13 and 14 are the ones most often copied without a fact behind them. See `honest-adaptation.md`.
