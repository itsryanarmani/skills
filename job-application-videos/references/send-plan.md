# What to send, and what it costs

Nothing goes out without a go-ahead from the owner. Write the plan, show the cost,
and wait.

## Channels

| Channel | Cost (checked November 2026) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Postcard (PostGrid, 4x6) | about $0.90 each in the US and Canada, $1.23 to $2.84 in the UK, no published rate for France | The first card carries a QR code to the unlisted video. Check the price page on the day. |
| Email | free | Use a verified work email. A guessed address bounces and hurts the domain. |
| LinkedIn message | free | A short connection note carries the video link. Free accounts have a monthly cap on notes: check it. |
| X message | free | Only when the person's handle is known and the person posts there. |
| Gift (Goody) | a gift price, often $25 to $60, charged when accepted | Goody emails a link and the person picks and accepts. It needs the owner's account. |

## Gifts

Many companies bar staff from accepting gifts. A recruiter may have to refuse. Gift
only people who are not recruiters, only a few, and read the company's policy first.

## A sequence that is not pushy

1. Day 1: a LinkedIn note to the likely hiring leader, with the video link.
2. Day 3: a postcard to the same person.
3. Day 6: a postcard to one peer on the same team.
4. Optional: one gift to one person at the company that matters most.

Keep it to two or three people per company. For a small firm where everyone is a
decision maker, one card for each person is fine, spread over a few days.

## Cost line to show the owner

`cards x price`, by country, plus gifts if any. Example: 140 US and Canada cards at
$0.90 is $126, and at five cards a day that takes 28 days.

## Before the first send

- Every address to be mailed is `verified` or `corrected`, or the owner approved it by name.
- Each video is unlisted, and its link opens in a signed-out browser. The QR code decodes on a printed proof.
- A proof card is rendered in test mode and the owner approved the design.
- The `Outreach` tab is ready to log each send.
