# job-application-videos

One short video per role, shown to the right people, sent in more than one way.
This skill is the map. Each step names the tool that does the work. Nothing in
this skill sends or publishes anything on its own: every send, upload, thumbnail
change and privacy change needs the owner's go-ahead (see
[send-plan.md](references/send-plan.md)).

Measured November 2026 on a campaign of 28 roles and 27 videos.

## The steps

| # | Step | Tool | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | List the roles: company, role, posting link, the name in the file name | a sheet (`Roles` tab) | one row per role |
| 2 | Record one video per role, 40 to 90 s, name the company and one concrete fact | the phone or camera | `<role>.mov` |
| 3 | Clean the cut: dead air, loudness | `talking-head-edit` | `<role>-edited.mp4` |
| 4 | Add the look (name tag, logos, numbers, one visual at a time) | `talking-head-graphics`, [batch-edit-brief.md](references/batch-edit-brief.md) | final `.mp4` |
| 5 | Upload one video per role, same title pattern. Keep it private while you draft, then set it to unlisted before you send | YouTube Studio | a link per role |
| 6 | Make thumbnails | `youtube-thumbnails`, then `thumbnail-cutout` and `youtube-thumbnail-set` | a thumbnail per video |
| 7 | Research people and companies | [research-and-sheet.md](references/research-and-sheet.md) | `Contacts` and `Companies` tabs |
| 8 | Find and verify mailing addresses | `address-verify`, [addresses.md](references/addresses.md) | a verified address or an honest "unverified" |
| 9 | Plan what to send and what it costs | [send-plan.md](references/send-plan.md) | a schedule the owner approves |
| 10 | Send, one wave at a time, and log it | `agentic-postcard`, `agentic-campaign` | the `Outreach` tab |

Steps 2 to 6 can run in parallel with steps 7 and 8. Step 10 never starts before
the owner approves step 9.

## Rules that hold the campaign together

1. **The link must play for the person who gets it.** A private YouTube video plays
   only for Google accounts you invite, so a recipient sees "Video unavailable".
   Keep a video private while you draft. Before you send, set it to unlisted and open
   the link in a signed-out browser. An unlisted link works for anyone who has it,
   so send it only to the chosen person.
2. **One sheet is the source of truth.** Every fact has a source URL and a
   confidence (high, medium, low). A guess is marked as a guess.
3. **Public professional facts only.** Use a person's name, title and work
   address from public pages. Do not collect home addresses or personal phone
   numbers. Do not guess work emails and send to them unverified.
4. **Verify before you mail.** A postcard costs real money and a wrong address is
   lost money. Run `address-verify`, then read [addresses.md](references/addresses.md)
   for what each result means. Mail only `verified` or `corrected` addresses. Any
   other address is an exception the owner approves one by one.
5. **Gifts can hurt.** Many companies bar staff from accepting gifts. Check the
   policy and keep gifts for people who are not recruiters.
6. **Report what you did, not what you meant to do.** Say which cells you wrote,
   which tab, and how you checked.

Read [lessons.md](references/lessons.md) before you start. Each line cost time once.
