# Hook formulas for 20-30 second ads

The hook is the first 1 to 3 seconds: the spoken line, the first on-screen text and the first image, all at once. A viewer decides to stay or leave in that time.

These ten shapes are adapted from the hook structure in [sergebulaev/tiktok-skills](https://github.com/sergebulaev/tiktok-skills) (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Sergey Bulaev; the full notice is in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md)) and from reading the other public repos in [repos](repos.md). The wording here is rewritten, and the goals and rules are our own.

Each formula has a spoken line, an on-screen line and a first image. The spoken and on-screen lines differ. Fill the braces with real facts.

| # | Name | Best for | Spoken | On screen | First image |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Result first | Purchase | "{Result} in {time}. Here is how." | "{Result}, {time}" | The finished result, no intro |
| 2 | Pattern break | Lead | "Stop {common habit}. It works against you." | "{Habit}: wrong" | An odd object or move, then the host |
| 3 | One exact number | Purchase, saves | "{Odd precise number} {what it counts}." | The number, large | The number, then the proof |
| 4 | Open question | Lead | "Why does {surprise} happen? Here is the answer." | "{Surprise}: why?" | The host mid-gesture |
| 5 | Call-out | Lead, shares | "If you {specific behavior}, this is for you." | "POV: you {moment}" | The moment, acted out |
| 6 | Flat claim | Lead, comments | "{Claim, no hedge}." | The claim in 4-7 words | The host, still, to camera |
| 7 | Count promise | Saves | "{N} {things} that {payoff}." | "{N} {things}" | A counter "1/{N}" from second one |
| 8 | Mid-story | Completion | "I am in the worst part of {story} and then {stakes}." | "This almost {went wrong}" | The peak frame, then rewind |
| 9 | Skip the intro | Saves | "Here is how to {result} in {time}. Step one." | "How to {result}" | One flash of the result, then step one |
| 10 | Trend with a twist | Shares | "{Trend line}, but for {niche}." | "{Trend text} .. except {twist}" | The trend beat, then the twist |

Formula 8 needs a real story. A host with no life story cannot tell one, so skip it for an AI host.

## Pick by goal

- **Lead** (a free guide): 2, 4, 5, 6.
- **Purchase** (a paid kit): 1, 3, 9.
- **Saves and shares**: 3, 7, 10.
- **Comments**: 4, 6.

## Micro-rules

- The result or the tension is in frame one.
- Say it plain. A strange opening warns the viewer that a trick is coming.
- Open the loop in the hook. Close it in the proof beat.
- Make the last frame restart the loop.
- A hook that cannot be shown on screen in the first second is not a hook.

## Never

- Open on a greeting, a logo or a slow zoom.
- Read the post caption aloud as the hook.
- Promise a result you do not show.
- Use a trending sound that has nothing to do with your offer.
- Stack calls to action.
