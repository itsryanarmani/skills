# Checklists

Tick every box. A box you cannot tick is a finding to report, not a step to skip.

## Before you generate

- [ ] One sentence says the idea of the video and what the viewer should feel.
- [ ] The title is known and the thumbnail does not repeat it.
- [ ] Two or three reference photos: front view, different expressions, sharp.
- [ ] The person agreed to a generated likeness. They know the photos are uploaded.
- [ ] Cost is stated before `--yes` (about $0.07 per pose).

## Each pose

- [ ] Same person: face shape, hair, beard, clothes match the references.
- [ ] Hands have five fingers and sit in a natural place.
- [ ] Background is one flat colour with no gradient, text, logo or watermark.
- [ ] The person fills the right 45 percent. The left half is empty.
- [ ] The expression matches the line it will carry (see `tactics.md`).
- [ ] No second person, no odd prop, no glow or outline around the person.

## Each layout

- [ ] One focal point. One idea. At most four words.
- [ ] Text is bold, high contrast, and does not touch the person.
- [ ] All lines of one text block share one font size.
- [ ] Three colours at most.
- [ ] Bottom-right and bottom-left corners are clear (turn on the safe-zone view).
- [ ] The longest name in the batch still fits and stays readable.
- [ ] Passes the 120 px test: the name and the face are clear.
- [ ] Does not copy another creator's artwork.

## Export

- [ ] `thumbnail-export` printed `"ok": true` for every file.
- [ ] Size is exactly 1280x720. File is at most 2 MB. Type is PNG or JPEG.
- [ ] You opened three files at full size and read every word.
- [ ] One file viewed at 120 px still reads.
- [ ] A name is spelled the way the person wants it (a search is not proof).

## Upload (only when the owner says go)

- [ ] The channel account is verified for custom thumbnails.
- [ ] You are in the right channel. Check the channel name before each upload.
- [ ] The thumbnail goes to the right video (match by title and length).
- [ ] After upload, reload the video page and look at the thumbnail.
- [ ] A private or unlisted video stays private. A thumbnail change does not alter visibility.
